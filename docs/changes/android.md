- Pokémon Card GB2 runs on Android phones and tablets (Android 8 or newer) as a native app, the same as on the desktop: download the APK, open it to install, and choose your ROM when the app asks. The console's buttons are drawn on the screen, below the picture in portrait like a Game Boy and beside it in landscape; a gamepad or keyboard works too. Battery saves, screenshots and settings are in the app's folder, which a computer can open over USB to copy a save in or out.
